The eleven player skins were supplied in ../travellerscompanions. Ten 64x64 skins are copied unchanged; the legacy 64x32 Varys skin is expanded to 64x64 using Minecraft's mirrored left-limb UV convention. Original files are preserved.
Better Combat, Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks and Common Weapons animation data is loaded from the installed mods at runtime, not redistributed.
Epic Knights equipment is resolved from the installed Epic Knights registry. No Epic Knights assets are redistributed.
Iron's, Simply Swords and Common Weapons equipment is resolved from installed registries. No third-party mod JARs, textures, models or decompiled sources are bundled.

Troops use their own supplied 64x64 skins: footsquire.png, footpaladin.png and footarcher.png. Each is copied byte-for-byte from ../travellerscompanions and verified against its SHA-256 digest.

Serious Player Animations walking keyframes are resolved from the installed mod's PlayerAnimationRegistry at runtime. No SPA assets or code are redistributed.
ParCool's owner FastRun capability is accessed through its installed classes. CompanionParkour and CompanionLocomotion implement original NPC movement and poses; ParCool player procedures and animation source are not bundled.
Integration references: https://github.com/alRex-U/ParCool and https://github.com/McVader34/Serious-Player-Animations
