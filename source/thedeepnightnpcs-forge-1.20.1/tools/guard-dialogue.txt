@|The watch|A quiet gate is a good day's work.
>|Any trouble?|Nothing I cannot keep an eye on from this post. That is not a promise about the entire road. Watch the dark approaches and do not mistake an empty path for a safe one.~If you bring danger back with you, leave room for people to move. A doorway full of panicked travellers is a poor place to swing a sword.
>|Where is Eldara?|Look for Eldara the Eternal Sage wherever the keeper of this refuge has placed her. She stands at her post and will turn to face you when you approach.~Right-click to speak. She has enough stories for a long stay and enough practical advice to make that stay optional. Ask about the Spire if you want to know what our watch is meant to protect.
>|Where is Caelen?|Caelen the Monster Hunter keeps the contract board at his post. Speak to him, choose Available hunts, and read a report before accepting it.~He pays in emeralds and experience for completed hunts. He also insists on your own credited kills, so do not expect my sword to do your bookkeeping for you.
>|Your patrol?|About five blocks from where I was stationed. Close enough to keep the post in sight, far enough to stretch my legs and notice someone trying to come around the side.~If the settlement grows, it will need another post. I cannot guard three gates by promising to run faster between them.
>|That shield?|It gives a person behind me another moment to get through the door. The sword is useful, but I would rather finish a watch with everyone present than with an impressive account of how far I chased something.~Stay clear of the swing if trouble comes close. I will deal with nearby hostiles within my post, then return to watching the ground I was given.
>|The Spire's rules?|Respect the guests, keep the approaches clear, and do not start fights beside the people trying to rest. If you see danger, give a useful warning rather than a dramatic one.~Beyond that, ask when you are uncertain. Most of us would rather explain a rule than discover how creatively someone misunderstood it.
>|How is the watch?|Quiet enough to hear my armour complain. The sage says that is a blessing. She has never worn this buckle through an entire evening.~Still, a quiet watch means somebody has time to eat, sleep, or mend something. I try to remember that when I begin wishing for an interesting story.
>|A word for the road|Keep your provisions close and your return route clearer than your boast. If the expedition goes badly, coming home is still an achievement.~We will be here when you return. Tell Caelen about the hunt, tell Eldara what you learned, and tell me if something followed you.
